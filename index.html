1. Introduction & Corporate Structure

What this company is, structured top-down, and what you're actually trying to build.

Corporate structure

  • RubiNexus Group (RubinexusGroup.com) — the parent holding company. It owns the intellectual property, the software, the bank accounts, and the brand rights. It exists so that anything you build later beyond freight (fintech tools, other software products) can sit under the same legal umbrella without restructuring everything.
  • Nexus Freight (NexusFreight.com) — the operating brand customers actually see and use. Everything freight-related — dispatch, load matching, driver tools — runs under this name.
Parent Domain Asset (RubinexusGroup.com Setup)
Initial registry fee

What you want to do

Build a freight technology company that starts as a dispatch/utility service and grows into a two-sided digital platform connecting shippers who need cargo moved with drivers/carriers who move it — while also making money in the background through financial-services partnerships (factoring, fuel cards) rather than relying only on a direct commission. The long-term ambition spans the US, Europe, and Canada, with a specific extension into oil-rig and industrial freight as a differentiated niche.

Vision, in detail

Nexus Freight becomes the dispatcher independent drivers actually trust — not because it's the biggest board, but because it's the most honest one: transparent commission (or genuinely free where promised), fast payment, a real person to talk to, and technology that removes busywork (invoicing, rate confirmations, tracking) instead of adding another app to check. On the shipper side, it becomes the reliable alternative to juggling multiple broker relationships for irregular freight — one dispatcher who knows their lanes and responds fast.

"The goal isn't to out-build DAT. It's to be the dispatcher a specific group of drivers and shippers trust more than DAT, in a specific place, for a specific kind of freight."

5. Growth, Driver Engagement & Enterprise Clients

Acquisition funnels, retention mechanics, and major client acquisition.

Driver Engagement Mechanics

  • Fast Payment Guarantee: 24–48 hour payout window, treated as non-negotiable.
  • Tiered Recognition: Bronze/Silver/Gold based on completed loads.
  • Referral Program: Conditioned bonus ($25–$100) per successful active driver referral.

Enterprise Client Sequencing

  1. Build track record with 10–20 small/mid shipper loads.
  2. Position as overflow/backup capacity provider.
  3. Meet strict insurance and safety documentation standards.

6. Risk Analysis & CEO Playbook

Identified business risks, mitigation protocols, and solo-founder operating cadence.

Critical Risk Mitigations

  • API/Data Blockade: Never architect core product around a single third-party data source.
  • Cash Flow Mismatch: Maintain a standing 1–2 month operating buffer, untouched by growth spend.
  • Regulatory Compliance: Paid legal consultation prior to launch to determine exact dispatch vs. broker liability.
