1. Introduction & Corporate Structure

What this company is, structured top-down, and what you're actually trying to build.

Corporate structure

  • RubiNexus Group (RubinexusGroup.com) — the parent holding company. It owns the intellectual property, the software, the bank accounts, and the brand rights.
  • Nexus Freight (NexusFreight.com) — the operating brand customers actually see and use for dispatch, load matching, and driver tools.

Vision, in detail

Nexus Freight becomes the dispatcher independent drivers actually trust — transparent commission, fast payment, a real person to talk to, and technology that removes busywork. Five years out, the vision is a recognizable name in specific verticals with a dedicated driver community and enterprise clients.

"The goal isn't to out-build DAT. It's to be the dispatcher a specific group of drivers and shippers trust more than DAT."

Backend Monetization
